# 🎬 Netflix Movies & TV Shows Dashboard | Tableau

An interactive **Netflix Movies & TV Shows Dashboard built with Tableau** to analyze Netflix content by type, ratings, release year, country, genre, duration, and date added.

## 🎯 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to transform Netflix content data into an interactive **Tableau Business Intelligence dashboard**.

The dashboard helps answer:
- How many Movies and TV Shows are available?
- What is the distribution between Movies and TV Shows?
- Which countries have the highest number of titles?
- Which ratings are most common?
- Which genres have the highest number of titles?
- How has Netflix content changed over the years?
- What are the release years of available content?
- How long are movies in the catalog?
- When were titles added to Netflix?

## 🏢 Business Problem

Streaming platforms have large and constantly growing content libraries. Analyzing thousands of titles manually makes it difficult to understand content distribution, audience categories, geography, and historical trends.

This dashboard provides an interactive solution to:
- Compare Movies vs TV Shows
- Analyze ratings and audience categories
- Identify popular genres
- Explore geographical content distribution
- Track content additions over time
- Analyze release-year trends
- Explore title-level details

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🎬 Movies & TV Shows Distribution

The dashboard compares:
- **Movies:** 4,265 (68.42%)
- **TV Shows:** 1,969 (31.58%)

Movies form the larger share of the analyzed Netflix catalog.

### 🌎 Total Movies & TV Shows by Country

A geographic map displays the distribution of Netflix titles across countries, providing a global view of the content library.

### ⭐ Rating Analysis

A bar chart compares titles by rating, including TV-MA, TV-14, TV-PG, R, PG-13, NR, PG, TV-Y7, TV-G, TV-Y and other ratings.

### 🏆 Top 10 Genres

A horizontal bar chart highlights the leading genre groups, including Documentaries, Stand-Up Comedy, International Dramas, Independent Dramas, Comedies, Kids' TV and Children & Family content.

### 📅 Total Movies & TV Shows by Years

An area chart shows title additions over time and highlights the strong growth in Netflix content in later years.

### 🔎 Title-Level Details

The dashboard provides details for selected titles:
- Title
- Type
- Rating
- Release Year
- Date Added
- Duration
- Genre
- Description

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard:
- **4,265 Movies** represent approximately **68.42%** of the analyzed titles.
- **1,969 TV Shows** represent approximately **31.58%**.
- Movies are the larger content type in this dataset.
- **TV-MA** is the most represented rating in the displayed analysis.
- Netflix titles are distributed across many countries globally.
- Documentaries and Stand-Up Comedy are among the leading genre groups shown.
- Content additions increase significantly in the later years, especially from around 2016 onward.
- The dashboard supports title-level exploration through interactive filtering.

> Insights are based on the dashboard preview and may change when filters are applied.
